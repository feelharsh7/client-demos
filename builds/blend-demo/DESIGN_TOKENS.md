# DESIGN_TOKENS.md — Blend Commerce Demo (Scientific DTC & Subscription Retention Archetype)

Extracted from live benchmarks: `seed.com` (Daily Synbiotic) & `mudwtr.com` (Ritual Starter Kit)

---

## 1. Palette & Surface Restraint
- **Primary Canvas**: `#F4F1EA` (Warm unbleached oat parchment) & `#FAF8F5` (Sterile lab alabaster)
- **Primary Ink / Structure**: `#15241C` (Deep bio-forest emerald ink) & `#1B1B1B` (Charcoal)
- **Accent Highlight**: `#34D399` (Bio-active cellular mint) & `#D4AF37` (Raw botanical gold)
- **Container Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` with hairline structural borders `1px solid rgba(21, 36, 28, 0.12)`
- **Packaging Aesthetic**: Forest-green glass apothecary Welcome Jar paired with 100% bio-compostable mycorrhizal paper refill pouches.

---

## 2. Typography Scale & Contrast
- **Display Headlines**: Clinical Editorial Serif / Grotesque (`Newsreader` / `Cormorant Garamond` + `Space Grotesk`)
  - Weight: 300 / 400 (Light / Book)
  - Tracking: `-0.02em` on headlines, tight and authoritatively scientific
  - Line-height: `1.08` – `1.15`
- **Body & Clinical Data**: Swiss Monospace & Grotesque (`Space Mono` & `Inter`)
  - Weight: 400 (Regular) / 600 (SemiBold)
  - Tracking: `+0.08em` on uppercase biological assay tags (`AFU viability`, `strain code`, `lot analysis`)
  - Line-height: `1.5`

---

## 3. Structural Rhythm & Retention Mechanics
- **Sustainable Starter Kit Flow**:
  - Month 1: Reusable emerald glass apothecary jar + travel glass vial + 30-day supply.
  - Month 2+: Zero-plastic bio-compostable refill pouches shipped flat in post-consumer craft mailers.
- **Interactive Retention Engine**:
  - "The Sustainable Membership" ($39/mo, Save 25% + Free Welcome Kit + Carbon-neutral free shipping) vs. "Single Box Trial" ($52).
  - Delivery cadence matrix (30 Days, 60 Days, 90 Days).
  - 1-click frictionless SMS pause/skip/cancel toggle directly visualized.
- **Anti-AI Guardrails Applied**:
  - No generic neon gradient pills or emojis.
  - Clinical cellular assay table with verified strain numbers (e.g., `NT-804`, `LM-311`, `LT-109`).
  - Tactile physical unboxing diagram and split-screen sticky purchase drawer.
