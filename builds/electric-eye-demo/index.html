The Physics of Cooking

Why Top Chefs Never Use Glass

Standard supermarket olive oil fails three fundamental culinary tests.

❌ Legacy Supermarket Bottle

  • · Uncontrolled Pouring: Wide glass necks glug unevenly, flooding delicate pans with too much oil or failing to dress a plate evenly.
  • · Greasy Slippery Sides: Drips inevitably run down the neck, coating the glass, staining cabinets, and causing dropped bottles.
  • · Bulk Blend Degradation: Clear or green glass exposes oil to fluorescent supermarket light, rapidly oxidizing fragile polyphenols.

✓ Picual & Co. Squeeze Bottle

  • ✓ Directional Micro-Dosing: Squeeze precisely where you want: a thin spiral over burrata or a broad fan across a cast-iron skillet.
  • ✓ Zero-Drip Precision Spout: Integrated shut-off valve cuts the stream the instant you release pressure. Clean counters forever.
  • ✓ UV-Opaque Food Grade Barrier: Blocks 100% of ambient light to keep freshly pressed olive oil vibrant and peppery down to the last drop.
