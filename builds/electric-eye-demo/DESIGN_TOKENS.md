# DESIGN_TOKENS.md — Electric Eye Demo (Taste-First DTC Archetype)

Extracted from live benchmarks: `graza.co` and `drinkolipop.com`

---

## 1. Palette & Surface Restraint
- **Primary Canvas**: `#F6E6D9` (Warm peach buttermilk) & `#FBF8F2` (Unbleached cream base)
- **Primary Ink / Structure**: `#3C422E` (Kalamata olive deep forest green)
- **High-Voltage Highlight**: `#D1E030` (Electric squeeze chartreuse) & `#A7E852` (Soda pop lime)
- **Paper Accent / Card Ground**: `#FFFFFF` with hairline olive border `1.5px solid #3C422E`
- **Offset Shadow Tone**: `#3C422E` (Solid 2px–3px offset tactile drop shadow; zero blurry diffuse drop-shadows)

---

## 2. Typography Scale & Contrast
- **Display Headlines**: Typewriter Serif / 70s Chunky Display Serif (GT Alpina Typewriter / Windsor feel via `DM Serif Display` + `Space Grotesk`)
  - Weight: 700 / 900
  - Tracking: `-0.02em` to `-0.03em`
  - Line-height: `1.05` to `1.15`
- **Body & Metadata**: Grotesque Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Space Grotesk`)
  - Weight: 500 (Medium), 700 (Bold)
  - Tracking: `+0.04em` on uppercase micro-labels (`10px`–`11px`)
  - Line-height: `1.45`

---

## 3. Structural Rhythm & Components
- **Button Architecture**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `16px 32px`, flat fill `#D1E030` or `#3C422E`, backed by tactile hard offset border `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #3C422E`.
- **Bundle & Selector Mechanics**:
  - Vertical radio card stack with solid `#3C422E` olive badges ("Most Popular", "Save $8").
  - Tactile squeeze bottles + aluminum refill can combos to drive average order value (AOV) and customer lifetime value (LTV).
  - Tilted sticker stamps (CSS `-2deg` to `+3deg` tilt).
  - Subscribe & Save toggle with cadence selector (Every 30, 60, 90 Days).
- **Asymmetric Layout**: Off-center product pour showcase, macro harvest details, hand-drawn vector accents, and sticky mobile purchase bar.
