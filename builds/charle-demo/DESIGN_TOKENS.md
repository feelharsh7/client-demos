# DESIGN_TOKENS.md — Charle Agency Demo (Quiet-Luxury Architectural Archetype)

Extracted from live benchmarks: `toteme.com` and `aesop.com`

---

## 1. Palette & Surface Restraint
- **Primary Canvas**: `#FFFEF2` (Aesop warm parchment linen) and `#F6F5E8` / `#F8F8F6` (Travertine alabaster stone)
- **Primary Ink / Typography**: `#252525` and `#111111` (Deep charcoal ink, never harsh 100% blue-black)
- **Architectural Hairlines**: `1px solid rgba(37, 37, 37, 0.12)` and `#333333`
- **Single Intentional Accent**: `#8E633C` (Raw umber botanical bronze) & `#A67C52` (Warm amber)
- **Border Radius**: Strict `0px !important` across all buttons, cards, containers, images, and inputs.

---

## 2. Typography Scale & Contrast
- **Display Headlines**: Literary Editorial Humanist Serif (Zapf Humanist / Toteme Sans feel via `Cormorant Garamond` & `Newsreader`)
  - Weight: 300 (Light) / 400 (Regular)
  - Tracking: `+0.01em` to `+0.03em`
  - Line-height: `1.15` to `1.25`
  - Style: Italicized editorial emphasis on key materials and origins.
- **Body & Metadata**: Authoritative Swiss Grotesque Sans (`Inter` / `Space Grotesk` with Swiss metrics)
  - Weight: 400 (Book) / 500 (Medium)
  - Tracking: `+0.06em` to `+0.12em` on uppercase category paths and provenance chips (`10px`–`11px`)
  - Middle-Dot Notation: `·` between category, product, and volume (e.g., `Atelier · 500 mL · Single-Harvest Cold Pressed`)

---

## 3. Structural Rhythm & Components
- **Architectural Hairline Grid**: Asymmetric 12-column split with razor-thin structural line dividers.
- **Whitespace Restraint**: Monumental, unhurried padding (`py-24` to `py-36`, `px-8` to `px-16`).
- **Product Presentation**: Amber apothecary glass, unpolished stone pedestals, clean architectural geometry, zero cheesy badges or urgency counters.
- **Quiet-Luxury Commerce Drawer**:
  - Flat rectangular wireframe or solid charcoal CTA with zero rounded corners.
  - Transparent pricing and ethical provenance transparency accordions.
  - Fixed tactile bottom bar: `£85.00 · Complimentary Global Courier` with direct 1-click cart addition.
