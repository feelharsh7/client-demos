# Lead Research Skill

Find 15 real, high-value B2B prospects (mid-market digital agencies or growing D2C Shopify brands) using web search and integrated browser tools, audit their current assets for conversion gaps, and log actionable cold outreach entries into `leads/prospects_today.md`.

## Target Profile Criteria
1. **D2C Shopify Brands**: Running paid ads, clear active traffic, but sub-optimal mobile hero/checkout UX, slow load times, or dated creative formatting.
2. **Growth/Creative Agencies**: Digital marketing, performance creative, or web design agencies looking for overflow white-label execution bandwidth.

## Workflow Instructions

### Step 1: Prospect Discovery
- Utilize search tools or the `browser` subagent to locate 15 target domains.
- Sources: Shopify brand directories, ad libraries (Meta Ad Library), LinkedIn company pages, agency directories (Clutch, Agency Vista), and curated brand collections.
- Filter for brands actively investing in customer acquisition where a 72-hour CRO revamp or video creative sprint offers immediate ROI.

### Step 2: Decision-Maker Identification
- Identify the relevant key stakeholder for each prospect:
  - D2C: Founder, Co-founder, Head of Growth, E-commerce Director, CMO.
  - Agencies: Founder, Managing Director, Creative Director, Head of Operations.
- Record name, title, and public contact handle/LinkedIn URL where available.

### Step 3: Rapid Asset Audit (UX / Video Flaw)
- Inspect the prospect's live website or recent ad creatives:
  - **Shopify/Landing Page CRO**: Spot 1 concrete, high-friction flaw (e.g., cluttered mobile above-the-fold, lack of sticky CTA, hidden value proposition, weak social proof above the fold, confusing multi-step checkout).
  - **Video Creative**: Spot 1 clear creative flaw (e.g., slow 3-second hook, missing dynamic captions, non-vertical aspect ratio, lack of clear CTA).

### Step 4: Craft the 3-Sentence Personalized Pitch
Draft a crisp, non-generic 3-sentence pitch:
- **Sentence 1 (Observation)**: Call out the exact flaw observed on their live page/ad with genuine appreciation for their brand.
- **Sentence 2 (Solution & Proof)**: Propose the specific 72-hour sprint fix and the exact uplift or asset delivery (e.g., rebuilt interactive hero preview or 15-pack video assets).
- **Sentence 3 (Low-Friction CTA)**: Offer a zero-risk, no-strings-attached preview (e.g., "I already rebuilt your hero section with a sticky buy flow—mind if I send over a 60-second video walkthrough?").

### Step 5: Save Output to `leads/prospects_today.md`
Format the output as a clean Markdown file in `leads/prospects_today.md` containing:
1. Summary table:
   | # | Brand / Agency | Website | Decision-Maker & Title | Core Flaw Identified | Pitch Ready? |
2. Detailed Breakdown section for each prospect with:
   - Full URL
   - Decision-Maker Profile
   - Specific Flaw Breakdown
   - 3-Sentence Cold Pitch
